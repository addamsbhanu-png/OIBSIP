\# FOHOWAY – Nourishing Body Lotion Landing Page



\## 1. Project Overview



The FOHOWAY Nourishing Body Lotion Landing Page is a modern, responsive product showcase website developed as part of the Oasis Infobyte Web Development Internship.



The website promotes FOHOWAY Nourishing Body Lotion with SPF-30. It presents product information through an elegant and attractive user interface, highlighting its moisturizing benefits, ingredients, product details, and skincare features.



The landing page is designed with a clean layout, soothing colors, attractive typography, and product imagery to provide visitors with a pleasant browsing experience.



\## 2. Objectives



\- To develop an attractive and user-friendly product landing page.

\- To showcase FOHOWAY Nourishing Body Lotion.

\- To present product benefits and ingredients clearly.

\- To create an engaging and responsive website.

\- To improve practical skills in frontend web development.



\## 3. Technologies Used



\- \*\*HTML5:\*\* Used to structure the website and its content.

\- \*\*CSS3:\*\* Used for styling, layouts, colors, typography, and responsive design.

\- \*\*JavaScript:\*\* Used for interactive website functionality, if implemented.



\## 4. Project Features



1\. \*\*Navigation Bar:\*\* Includes Home, Benefits, Ingredients, Product, and Contact sections.

2\. \*\*Hero Section:\*\* Introduces FOHOWAY Nourishing Body Lotion with an attractive heading and product description.

3\. \*\*Product Display:\*\* Showcases the body lotion product image and packaging.

4\. \*\*Product Information:\*\* Highlights SPF-30 protection, 100 ml product size, and daily body-care use.

5\. \*\*Benefits Section:\*\* Presents the product's moisturizing and skincare highlights.

6\. \*\*Ingredients Section:\*\* Provides a dedicated section for product ingredients.

7\. \*\*Call-to-Action Buttons:\*\* Includes Explore the Product, Discover Ingredients, and Discover Product buttons.

8\. \*\*Elegant Design:\*\* Uses a soft cream background, green buttons, and golden accents for a premium skincare appearance.



\## 5. Product Details
